# Camera Error Handling

## Purpose

Give the crew member clear guidance when a storage image cannot be captured.

## Situations to handle

- The camera is not connected or cannot be opened.
- The camera cannot capture an image.
- The image is too dark, blurry, or does not show the storage area clearly.

## Messages and recovery steps

- **Camera unavailable:** “The camera is unavailable. Check that it is connected, then try again.”
- **Capture failed:** “The image could not be captured. Try again.”
- **Image unclear:** “The storage area is not clear in this image. Adjust the camera or lighting and retake it.”

The crew member should be able to retry the scan or enter the supply information manually if the camera remains unavailable.
