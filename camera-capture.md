# Camera Capture Feature

## Purpose

Capture a clear image of a spacecraft storage area so the supply tracker can help identify and count visible items.

## How a crew member would use it

1. Open the supply tracker and choose **Scan storage**.
2. Point the camera toward the storage area and capture an image.
3. Review the image to make sure the shelves and supplies are visible.
4. Retake the image if it is blurry, too dark, or incomplete.
5. Continue to the supply review when the image is suitable.

## Expected behavior

- Tell the crew member when the camera is ready.
- Provide a clear way to capture or retake an image.
- Ask the crew member to review the image before continuing.
- Keep the captured image associated with the inventory scan.


## Image quality tip

Good lighting and an unobstructed view help the tracker identify supplies more clearly.
